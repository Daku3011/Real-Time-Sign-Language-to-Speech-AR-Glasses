## **CATEGORY 2: ACADEMIC RESEARCH PROJECTS (Sign Language Recognition with AR Glasses)**

### 4. **SignGlass** (Penn State University, 2025)
- **What it does:** ASL recognition and translation to English using wearable glasses
- **Technology:** Three cameras (1 front, 2 side) capture manual + non-manual markers
- **Innovation:** First glasses-only system (no additional devices needed)
- **Performance:** WER=16.98%, BLEU1=83% accuracy
- **Study:** 14 Deaf participants
- **Status:** Research prototype (published September 2025)
- **Limitation:** Research-only, not commercially available
- **Unique Feature:** Captures facial expressions and body language
- **Ref:** SignGlass uses three wearable cameras for comprehensive capture of manual and non-manual ASL markers

### 5. **TransASL** (Academic Research, 2023)
- **What it does:** ASL recognition using smart glasses + earbuds
- **Technology:** Eyeglasses with outward/inward microphones and speakers
- **Performance:** 8.3% WER (words), 7.1% WER (sentences) for 80 words, 40 sentences
- **Limitation:** **User-dependent** (requires training per person), requires earbuds (not socially acceptable in deaf community)
- **Status:** Research prototype only
- **Ref:** TransASL uses customized eyeglasses with parallel sensing modalities for manual and non-manual markers

### 6. **ASL-Gesture-Projection for AR Glasses** (NSF-Funded Research)
- **What it does:** Speech-to-ASL pose translation displayed on AR glasses
- **Purpose:** Helps sign language users/learners translate speech to ASL
- **Technology:** Speech Recognition → Text → ASL Gloss → ASL Poses
- **Performance:** BLEU score 66.58, 1.825ms per gloss translation
- **Direction:** OPPOSITE of your project (speech→ASL, not ASL→speech)
- **Status:** Research proof-of-concept
- **Ref:** Implementation translates audio to text to ASL gloss to ASL poses for AR glasses display
